How it works

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather on Telegram.
  2. Add its token in the SIGMA BotHost panel — the bot goes live instantly.
  3. Users who start your bot appear in your panel automatically.
  4. Any time you want, broadcast a message to every user of every bot.
